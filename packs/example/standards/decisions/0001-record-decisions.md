# 0001: Record decisions

**Date:** 2026-01-15
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Decisions made in chat and pull requests get lost.

## Decision

Record decisions that are hard to reverse in `standards/decisions/`, numbered, with Context, Decision,
Alternatives considered and Consequences.

## Alternatives considered

- **Pull request descriptions** - hard to find later.

## Consequences

New engineers and agents can see why things are the way they are.
